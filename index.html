Your address decides your assigned school

Seattle Public Schools assigns each student to an attendance area school by home address. Families can also ask for a different school, including the option schools shown as diamonds, through Open Enrollment for School Choice. Those requests depend on space.

      Data sources

      Attendance areas, option school GeoZones and school locations from Seattle Public Schools’ public map services. Test results from the Washington State Report Card data (OSPI). Basemap and address search from King County GIS.

      Before you start

      • This is a test, not a finished site: an experiment in running the NYC School Zones map on Seattle’s data. It isn’t made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Seattle Public Schools.
      • Attendance areas come from Seattle Public Schools’ public map data (2025-26). Test scores are from spring 2026, from the Washington State Report Card. Data can be out of date or have errors.
      • Attendance areas change from year to year, and blocks near a boundary are easy to misread on a map.

      Always confirm an exact address with the district’s Address Lookup before you rely on it.

      Terms & privacy

      Use at your own risk. This map is provided as is, for general information only. It isn’t official enrollment, legal or educational advice, and it may contain errors. Don’t rely on it alone for any decision.

      Data. School data belongs to its sources (Seattle Public Schools and Washington State). The map’s code is open source under the AGPL-3.0.

      Privacy. There are no accounts, no cookies and no ads. Nothing you type is stored by this site. A few display settings, like whether you’ve seen this note, are kept only in your own browser.

      • Addresses you search are sent to King County GIS’s address locator to find the location.
      • Map tiles load from King County, the map library from Cloudflare’s cdnjs and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those servers see your IP address.
